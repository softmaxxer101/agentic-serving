Key Log Insights & Architectural Findings

Deep-dive conclusions synthesized from vllm.log, trie.log, and dmon.csv telemetry.

Prefix Caching Lever

Enabling prefix caching (E6, c4) yielded a 2.5× speedup on growing-context workloads, reducing TTFT from 3.9s down to 1.3s and boosting trie hit rates to 97.4%.

MTP Speculation Overhead

Multi-Token Prediction showed parity at c1 (29.3 tok/s), but regressed by ~28% at c8 due to low draft acceptance rates (38-44%) and KV pool memory pressure.

Concurrency Saturation

Burst workloads at c64/c128 hit saturation around c=8. At c32+, KV pool reaches 100%, triggering 11+ preemptions and dropping cache hit rates to ~3%.

Chunked Prefill Benefits

Chunked prefill enabled smooth interleaving of long-context requests, preventing head-of-line blocking and stabilizing inter-token latency under high concurrency tiers.
