Log Analysis & Key Findings Summary

1. Prefix Caching Efficiency (E6)

Enabling prefix caching provides a massive 2.5× throughput boost on growing-context workloads and 2.1× on burst requests. Mean TTFT dropped from 3.9s down to 13.8s without caching.

2. MTP Speculative Decoding Overhead (E1, E18)

Draft acceptance rates hovered at 38–44% (below the 79–90% target). At concurrency c=8, MTP caused a 28% drop in output tok/s and increased inter-token latency due to KV pool pressure.

3. Concurrency Saturation Knee (E4, E15)

System saturation occurs at c=8 on burst traffic. Beyond c=32, the KV pool hits 100% utilization, preemption spikes, and prefix-cache hit rates collapse near 0%.

4. Optimal Batch Token Budget (E14)

bt=8192 is the optimal default for the 2× RTX 3060 setup. Pushing to bt=16384 causes VRAM pinning at 32GB, thermal throttling at 81°C, and lower overall throughput (28.2 tok/s vs 41.3 tok/s).
